### 7. Frank Kern — "Double, Triple, or Even Quadruple Your Business" + Facebook retargeting ads

### 8. Billy Gene Is Marketing — the "Piss Them Off" agency video-ad script
- **Source URL(s):** https://www.digitalmarketer.com/blog/video-ad-formula-billy-gene/ ; https://www.creativelive.com/class/creating-effective-paid-ads-on-facebook-billy-gene/lessons/ad-copy-example

- **Headline:** (video-first ad; script hook is the headline)
- **Ad copy / script (verbatim where available):**
  > Part 1 — "Piss them off" (name the villain, described): open by voicing the prospect's frustration — for agency clients the villain is Facebook itself: "my customers are not on Facebook," "people don't click on Facebook ads and they most definitely don't buy on Facebook ads."
  >
  > Part 2 — the switch (verbatim): "But can I ask you an honest question? Is it possible that it's not a Facebook error, but it's a user error?"
  >
  > Part 3 — the solution (verbatim): "Advertising should be profitable not an expense, and it definitely shouldn't be complicated. So would you like me to run your ads for you? I offer a money back guarantee and more importantly, I guarantee quality leads that actually buy."
  >
  > Part 4 — CTA (verbatim): "Click the button and I'll send you some customers for free to earn your trust."
- **CTA:** "Click the button" → lead form / trial offer
