## Sweep notes
- **What worked:** WebSearch excerpt-mining was the only viable channel — the sandbox egress proxy hard-blocked WebFetch and curl (CONNECT 403) for nearly every target domain, including web.archive.org, clairepells.com, katelynjames.com, stevendecuba.com, learn.jailong.co. Exact-phrase searches against known funnel URLs recovered near-verbatim funnel copy anyway. Ad-specific URLs slugs ("/kj-all-access-ad", "/free-class-posing-reg", "-expired" bonus pages) were reliable tells of active paid campaigns.
- **Best verbatim wins:** the Katelyn James Facebook video post text (entry 1) — true ad-adjacent social copy with a signup count baked in; Book More Weddings Summit affiliate copy (entry 7); Wedding Academy bonus-stack copy (entry 12).
- **Dead ends:** foreplay.co / swipe-file sites (swiped.co, adangles.com, copyposse.com, medium.com all proxy-blocked); Facebook Ad Library itself (JS-only, and Meta MCP unavailable per session constraints); no documented Aisle Planner or ShootProof paid ads found; no verbatim primary-text capture for Jasmine Star's ads (case study exists but full page unfetchable); Taylor Jackson "Book More Weddings" course found but with only thin copy ("helped over 2000 photographers worldwide book more weddings"), left out of the numbered list.
- **Total entries:** 15 (verbatim-partial: 1; described: 2; funnel copy/partial: 12).
