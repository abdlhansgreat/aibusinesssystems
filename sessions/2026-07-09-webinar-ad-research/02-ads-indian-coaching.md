## Sweep notes
- **What worked:** `site:facebook.com "<name>" webinar/masterclass videos` was the highest-yield pattern — Google-indexed Facebook video/post pages expose the verbatim title + first ~140 chars of ad copy even though facebook.com itself was unfetchable. Exact-phrase searches (e.g. "Join the 4-hour online Business Breakthrough Seminar…", "Join our paid workshop FREE", "eLearning is HOT") extended several snippets and surfaced duplicate creatives (a strong winner signal). Trustpilot/Quora/review posts corroborated GrowthSchool/Outskill FOMO mechanics.
- **What was blocked:** WebFetch/curl returned 403 (gateway policy) for facebook.com (incl. Ad Library), grapevine.in, toption.org, substack.com, digitalazadi.com, devgadhvi10x.com, swipe-file domains (swiped.co, copyposse.com, foreplay/gethookd-type sites) — so no full-body ad copy could be pulled; everything verbatim here is partial by truncation, not by absence of the ad.
- **No documented copy found for:** Sandeep Maheshwari (does not run paid webinar ads — free-model brand, confirmed nothing found), Aditya Sharma (could not disambiguate/no ad copy indexed), BeYounick (entertainment, no coaching-webinar ads found), specific Ankur Warikoo *webinar* ads (his ads are course-checkout style), Hindi-only "फ्री मास्टरक्लास" ads (facebook Hindi post titles are poorly indexed; only Digital Azadi surfaced).
- **Hinglish hook coverage:** Digital Azadi's "FREE, LIVE और हिंदी में! क्या सीखोगे…" is the cleanest documented Hinglish structure (English trigger words + Hindi promise + क्या सीखोगे bullet lead-in); Rajiv Talreja's "Hyderabaaad, Are YOU READY?!" shows the vernacular-energy pattern in English ads.
- **Totals:** 15 entries; 13 with verbatim (partial) copy, 2 leaning on verbatim title + described body (entries 7, 12). 6 advertisers show duplicate-creative/longevity winner signals; 3 have third-party spend/scale documentation.
