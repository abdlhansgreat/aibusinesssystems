# 05 — 10 Hinglish Ad Scripts for Indian Wedding Club

**Session:** 2026-07-09 webinar-ad-research · **Deliverable**
**For:** Meta (Facebook/Instagram) video ads inviting Indian wedding business owners to the free Indian Wedding Club masterclass/webinar.
**Language:** Hinglish (aap, never tum). **Length:** ~45–75 sec each. **Format:** talking-head / story video (video beats static — Jasmine Star finding).
**Every script maps to a distinct hook type** from `04-hook-analysis.md` so you can A/B different angles.

> Placeholders to fill before shooting: `[HOST NAME]`, `[DATE/DAY]`, `[TIME]`, `[REGISTER LINK]`, `[RESULT STUDENT NAME]`, `[CITY]`. Keep the CTA line identical across all 10 for clean testing, or vary deliberately.

## Script 1: "Wedding Photographers…" (Audience Callout)
**Inspired by:** Alex Hormozi "Gym Owners…" + Arjun Kartha "Freelancers, beginners and pros."
**Angle:** Direct callout / relevance.

**[HOOK — 0:00–0:03]**
*(Host looks straight into camera, points)*
> "Wedding photographers, planners, decorators, makeup artists — haan, aap. Do minute rukiye."

**[BODY]**
> "Aap talent mein kisi se kam nahi. Phir bhi har mahine same struggle — leads kam, budget clients zyada, aur off-season mein toh phone hi nahi bajta.
> Problem aapke kaam ki nahi hai. Problem yeh hai ki aapko kabhi wedding business ko *business* ki tarah chalana sikhaaya hi nahi gaya.
> Indian Wedding Club ki is FREE masterclass mein main aapko wahi system dikhaunga jisse India ke top wedding vendors premium clients attract karte hain."

**[CTA]**
> "[DATE] ko LIVE. Seats limited hain. Neeche diye link se abhi FREE register karein."

**On-screen text:** "Wedding pros — yeh aapke liye hai" → "FREE Masterclass" → "Register 👇"
**Suggested first-frame visual:** Host pointing at camera, bold text "WEDDING PHOTOGRAPHERS…" filling the frame.

## How to test these (quick guide)
- **Run 3–4 at a time** as separate ads in one ad set; kill the losers after ~3–4 days, scale the winner.
- **First 3 seconds is 80% of the battle** — the on-screen text + first spoken line must land the hook instantly. Caption everything (most watch on mute).
- **Angles covered:** callout (1), seasonal pain (2), curiosity (3), personal offer (4), peer (5), scarcity (6), contrarian (7), aspiration (8), story (9), quantified (10). Rotate winners into new creative, don't just re-run.
- **CTA discipline:** keep the register link + "seats limited" consistent so you're testing the HOOK, not the CTA.
- **Production note:** shoot as talking-head/story video, vertical (9:16) for Reels/Stories + a 1:1 cut for feed.
