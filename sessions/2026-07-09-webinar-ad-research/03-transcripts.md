# 03 — Transcripts (Hook-Breakdown Format)

**Session:** 2026-07-09 webinar-ad-research
**Format:** metadata → verbatim copy → HOOK / PROBLEM-AGITATION / STORY-PROOF / OFFER / CTA → why it works
**Evidence note:** verbatim copy is `(partial)` where the source index truncated the ad, `(described)` where only structure was documented. Video ads could not be transcribed via an STT actor this session (Apify blocked), so entries labelled `(copy-only)` use the ad's published primary text/caption as the transcript. Sources are in the matching `02-ads-*.md` files.

## A. INDIAN COACHING / WEBINAR

### 1. Siddharth Rajsekar — "eLearning is HOT" (copy-only)
- **Source category:** Indian coaching · **Media:** video (copy-only)
- **Winner signals:** same body re-run under 3+ creatives; one free-webinar video 3.1M+ views; whole ILH business (30,000+ students) runs off this funnel.
- **Verbatim transcript / ad copy:**
  > 🙋🏻‍♂️ eLearning is HOT 🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥 Imagine being able to take your "existing knowledge" and package them into irresistible online courses for people to… *(partial)*
- **Breakdown:**
  - **HOOK (first 3 sec):** "eLearning is HOT 🔥" — trend/opportunity callout + emoji energy. Names the wave and implies you're missing it.
  - **PROBLEM/AGITATION:** implicit — you already have knowledge but it's trapped, unmonetised.
  - **STORY/PROOF:** "Imagine being able to…" — future-pacing the transformation instead of proof upfront.
  - **OFFER:** free webinar teaching how to package knowledge into online courses in 90 days.
  - **CTA:** Register for FREE Webinar (sidz.co shortlink).
- **Why it works:** Opportunity-led hook + "imagine" future-pacing lets the viewer self-identify ("I have knowledge too"). The 90-day timebound makes the dream concrete.
