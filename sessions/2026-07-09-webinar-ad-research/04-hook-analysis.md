## 1. Hook taxonomy (with what we observed)

| # | Hook type | What it does | Best examples in this research |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Audience callout** | Names the exact person so the right viewer stops | "Gym Owners…" (Hormozi #17); "Freelancers, beginners and pros" (Arjun Kartha #24) |
| 2 | **Shock statistic** | A hard number that triggers loss-aversion | "80% of coaches failed within 3-4 years" (Dev Gadhvi #2) |
| 3 | **Question-stack** | Rapid questions force internal answers | "Why do you go to work every day? …Why were you even born?" (Dev Gadhvi #3) |
| 4 | **Segmentation ("some… some…")** | Every viewer finds their bucket | "Some are struggling… some have a stable business but…" (Talreja #5) |
| 5 | **Curiosity gap + assumption-defeat** | Promises the answer isn't what you think | "Where 90% of your distractions come from *hint: not Netflix*" (Mindvalley #16) |
| 6 | **Oddly-specific income proof** | Precise number reads as real, not hype | "$17,947 per day" (Brunson #11); "$20,000,000 by age 27" (Ovens #10) |
| 7 | **"Would you like me to personally…?"** | Escalating benefit + personal + free | "…Double, Triple, or Even Quadruple Your Business …For Free?" (Kern #14) |
| 8 | **Peer / insider ("[niche] to [niche]")** | Makes a mass ad feel 1:1 | "quilter to quilter / yogi to yogi" (Porterfield #13) |
| 9 | **Value-reversal + scarcity** | Paid → free for a capped number | "Join our PAID workshop FREE. Only for the first 1000." (GrowthSchool #6) |
| 10 | **Villain/contrarian** | Makes the viewer's current method the enemy | "Websites are dead" (Brunson #11); "is it a Facebook error or a user error?" (Billy Gene #15) |
| 11 | **Story-lead (in-scene)** | Drop the viewer inside a scene, no pitch | "It was 8:30 AM. Again!!!" (Success Gyan #9) |
| 12 | **Quantified skill outcome** | Turns a vague skill into a number | "15 poses in just 5 minutes" (Katelyn James #20) |
| 13 | **Dream + fear parenthetical** | Aspiration with the exact fear named in ( ) | "Get booked out with clients you love (even when things are slow)" (Book More Weddings #22) |
| 14 | **Opportunity/trend callout** | "This is HOT right now, you're missing it" | "eLearning is HOT 🔥" (Rajsekar #1) |
| 15 | **Borrowed authority / event** | Big name + once-off live framing | "Free LIVE Training by Tony Robbins… that will break the internet" (#12) |
| 16 | **Small-cost dream** | Big outcome pinned to a tiny number | "consistent inquiries for as little as €5/day" (de Cuba #21); "₹99" (Talreja #4) |
| 17 | **Bandwagon social proof** | "Everyone's already in" | "Close to 5,000 of you have signed up" (Katelyn James #19) |

## 2. Anatomy of a winning webinar-invite ad

Across all three categories the winners share the same skeleton:

1. **HOOK (0–3s):** one of the 17 types above. Almost always either an audience callout, a shock/curiosity line, or a story-lead. For video, ≤12 words spoken/on-screen.
2. **PROBLEM/AGITATION:** name ONE sharp, specific pain (find clients / inconsistent inquiries / editing time / slow season). One pain, not five.
3. **STORY or PROOF:** either a personal rags-to-result story (Ovens, Success Gyan) OR a proof number (Hormozi, WedMeGood). Indian ads lean number + scale; Western ads lean personal story.
4. **OFFER:** the free (or ₹99) webinar/masterclass, with a concrete promise of what you'll learn — often a numbered list ("7 activities," "3 secrets," "10 hacks").
5. **CTA + urgency:** register/reserve + a scarcity device (limited seats, city countdown, "spots are limited," "first 1000").

**Length:** winning video ads run ~45–90 seconds. **Format finding:** Jasmine Star's data — *video beat static across almost all audiences*; produce these as talking-head/story video first.

## 3. India-specific patterns

- **Hinglish trigger-stack (the key formula):** English carries the marketing punch words (**FREE, LIVE, WEBINAR, PROFITABLE, BONUS**); Hindi carries the emotion and the verb (**और हिंदी में, क्या सीखोगे, Register करें, आज ही**). Template: `[English trigger] + [Hindi promise] + [English CTA verb + करें]`. (Digital Azadi #8.)
- **₹ tripwire pricing:** ₹99 "workshops/seminars" (Talreja, Dev Gadhvi, Success Gyan) filter for intent while staying frictionless. A pure-free webinar and a ₹99 workshop are both valid front ends.
- **Lakh/city stat-stacking:** Indian credibility currency = numbers in **lakhs** and **cities/states** ("5 lakh couples, 1 lakh+ vendors, 200+ cities" — WedMeGood). Western ads stack $; Indian ads stack reach.
- **Vernacular energy:** "Hyderabaaad, Are YOU READY?!" (Talreja) — city name + high-energy call works in India.
- **Guarantee to kill risk:** no-questions-asked refund on the ₹99 (Talreja) removes the last objection for a price-sensitive audience.
- **Respect register:** use **"aap"**, never "tum," when speaking to business owners.

## 4. The 12 most stealable hooks for Indian Wedding Club

Adapted to wedding-business owners (the raw source is in brackets):

1. **"Wedding photographers… "** [audience callout — Hormozi] — naked callout so only the right vendor stops.
2. **"Har wedding season aata hai, chala jaata hai — aur aapki income wahi ki wahi. Kyun?"** [shock/segmentation — Dev Gadhvi] — names the seasonal-income pain.
3. **"90% wedding businesses jahaan atakte hain, woh reason aapko lagta hai woh nahi hai. *Hint: yeh pricing nahi hai.*"** [curiosity + assumption-defeat — Mindvalley].
4. **"Kya main aapko personally dikhaun, bina paise kharch kiye, apni wedding business ko double kaise karein?"** [Kern "would you like me to personally…"].
5. **"Photographer se photographer ki baat — ek secret bata raha hoon."** [Porterfield "[niche] to [niche]"].
6. **"Yeh PAID masterclass, aaj FREE — sirf pehle [X] wedding business owners ke liye."** [GrowthSchool value-reversal + scarcity].
7. **"Aap sochte hain zyada shaadiyan chahiye. Sach yeh hai — aapko BEHTAR clients chahiye."** [villain/contrarian — Billy Gene].
8. **"Get booked out with the couples you love — off-season mein bhi."** [Book More Weddings dream + fear].
9. **"Subah 6 baje uthke, poore din shoot, raat 2 baje tak editing — aur mahine ke end mein wahi ₹40,000. Yeh kahani jaani-pehchaani lagti hai?"** [story-lead — Success Gyan].
10. **"India ki har badi wedding business ek hi cheez alag karti hai…"** [opportunity/authority — Rajsekar/Ovens].
11. **"Ek ₹5-lakh wedding aur ek ₹50,000 wedding — kaam same, farq sirf ek cheez ka."** [quantified — Katelyn James "15 in 5"].
12. **"FREE, LIVE aur Hindi mein — Indian Wedding Club Masterclass."** [Digital Azadi Hinglish trigger-stack].
