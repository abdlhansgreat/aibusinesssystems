## Adapted for Indian Wedding Club (Hinglish) — session 2026-07-09

1. **"Wedding photographers… "** (audience callout)
2. **"Har wedding season aata hai, chala jaata hai — aur aapki income wahi ki wahi. Kyun?"** (seasonal-income pain)
3. **"90% wedding businesses jahaan atakte hain, uski wajah woh nahi jo aapko lagti hai. *Hint: yeh pricing nahi hai.*"** (curiosity + assumption-defeat)
4. **"Kya main aapko personally dikhaun — bina extra paisa lagaye — apni wedding business double kaise karein?"** (would-you-like-me-to-personally)
5. **"Photographer se photographer ki baat — ek secret bata raha/rahi hoon."** ([niche] to [niche])
6. **"Yeh PAID masterclass, aaj FREE — sirf pehle [X] wedding business owners ke liye."** (value-reversal + scarcity)
7. **"Aapko lagta hai zyada shaadiyan chahiye. Sach yeh hai — aapko BEHTAR clients chahiye."** (contrarian)
8. **"Un couples se booked-out ho jao jinke saath kaam karna pasand hai — off-season mein bhi."** (dream + fear)
9. **"Subah 6 baje uthke poore din shoot, raat 2 baje tak editing — aur mahine ke end mein wahi ₹40,000. Jaani-pehchaani kahani?"** (story-lead)
10. **"Ek ₹5-lakh wedding aur ek ₹50,000 wedding — kaam same, farq sirf ek cheez ka."** (quantified contrast)
11. **"India ki har top wedding business ek hi cheez alag karti hai…"** (opportunity/authority curiosity)
12. **"FREE, LIVE aur Hindi mein — Indian Wedding Club Masterclass."** (Hinglish trigger-stack)
